Logistics Measurement Methodology

Explanation of the conversions and statistical treatment applied to the DummyJSON product information.

1. Shipping Information Conversion

Shipping estimates are converted into approximate calendar days to provide a consistent numerical measurement across product categories.

API Shipping Information Numeric Representation
Ships overnight 1 calendar day
Ships in 1-2 business days 2.1 calendar days
Other business-day estimates 1.4 calendar days per business day
Other day estimates 1 calendar day per day

The 1.4-day conversion is an analytical approximation. The 2.1-day value for the 1-2 business-day example is the specified approximation for that particular range. Neither represents an actual delivery-time measurement.

2. Warranty Information Conversion

Numeric warranty periods are standardized into days. This allows warranty duration to be compared with product ratings and prices.

API Warranty Information Numeric Representation
No warranty 0 days
1 week warranty 7 days
1 month warranty 30 days
1 year warranty 365 days
Lifetime warranty Excluded from numeric median

Lifetime warranties are counted separately and presented in their own scatterplots. They are not assigned an arbitrary numeric duration.
